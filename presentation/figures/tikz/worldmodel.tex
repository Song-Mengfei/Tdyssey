% How the structured world model predicts one step (deck slide "How the world model works"; also usable in an article).
% Facts: docs/LEARNING_FINDINGS.md section 4 (wm2): the pusher's controller and the exact pusher-to-letter distance are
% copied from the simulator, a contact gate keeps an untouched letter still, and a small network in the letter's own
% frame learns the letter's motion in contact; trained on the original policy's rollouts with noise on its targets.
% Overlay steps (\wmstep{n}{...} = \onslide<n->{...} in beamer): 1 inputs and output, 2 the copied parts, 3 the learned
% part and its data, 4 the repeat loop. Size 144 x 52 mm; fixed font sizes in the current family.
\makeatletter%

\makeatother%
