% How the state world model predicts one step (deck slide "How the state world model works"; also in the paper).
% Facts: docs/LEARNING_FINDINGS.md section 4 (wm2): the pusher's controller and the exact pusher-to-letter distance are
% copied from the simulator (tagged "copied"), a contact gate keeps an untouched letter still, and a small network in the
% letter's own frame learns the letter's motion in contact ("learned"); trained on the original policy's rollouts with
% noise on its targets. Overlay steps (\wmstep{n}{...} = \onslide<n->{...} in beamer): 1 inputs and output, 2 the copied
% parts and the legend, 3 the learned part and its data, 4 the repeat loop. Size 144 x 67 mm; fixed font sizes.
