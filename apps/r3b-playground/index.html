Model, physics and recording details

HMF500 is the default: it has the highest mean four-step IoU on the same O/C/B validation clips. Different horizons favor different models; accuracy across all letters and scenes is not established. T/L/X were not revalidated in this screen. Physics remains the original 100 Hz, 10 substeps per control, solver 10, original PD kp100/kv20, kinematic pusher and damping 0, without physics repairs.

H1 uses four actual history frames at every control step. H4 fixes the target for four controls per batch, feeds back raw float32 predictions, and restarts from actual history for the next batch. Moving the pointer during a batch changes only the red preview cross; the batch's requested target stays fixed. The actual pusher follows the original PD controller. Raw records retain both requested targets and actual endpoints/velocities. Review buttons distinguish the displayed step from the current scene step.

"Save diagnostic clip" saves actual model inputs, actions, seeds, actual RGB, raw floating-point predictions, and body/controller states including linear/angular velocities from the bounded ring buffer. These are not added to training data. Older clips beyond the ring capacity are evicted and explicitly marked. Contact-solver caches and hidden position-bias velocities are not serialized; bitwise-identical contact physics after restoration is not guaranteed.

"Start recording" captures the currently visible paired canvases, letter/mode/step/time labels and requested/preview targets. It does not capture the desktop or microphone. Releasing the pointer pauses pushing while recording continues. Letter, scene and mode changes and frame reviews appear in the video and event record. H4 intermediate frames that were not actually displayed are not invented. Starting a recording incrementally saves a compressed, complete raw batch record under the same recording ID, independently of the recent-clip ring. Stopping produces a WebM video and complete raw index.

Video chunks are buffered in this page with a 64 MiB limit; raw batch records have a separate 128 MiB budget. When a budget is insufficient, execution stops before the next batch and saves the captured video normally; this is not a model runtime limit. A failed save retains a downloadable video Blob; do not refresh. Raw model predictions are clipped/rounded to PNG only for display, without hole filling, fragment removal, sharpening or template replacement.
