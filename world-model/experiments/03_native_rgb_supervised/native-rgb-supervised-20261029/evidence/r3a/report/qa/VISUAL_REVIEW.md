# R3A recorded-data visual review

Root inspected all seven overview sheets (35 distinct representative clips),
the six last actual failed states and all three full-library scientific figures.
The figures have readable axes, units, legends and complete labels.

The strips show real object translation, positive and negative rotation,
withdrawal/recontact and boundary stalls across all six seen letters. Fixed-seed
random examples and the least-obvious-motion examples remain visible. Some
segments are stationary or move below the fixed H1 thresholds; the gallery does
not claim sustained large movement through every control. All requested video
roles have actual threshold/contact support. The O and B holes and the original
stepped polygon edges are visible; no appearance repair or shape warp was used.

Successful clips contain all49 control observations at10fps (simulation
timestamps0.0–4.8s). Failed attempts remain short; their last actual100Hz state,
including a partial failed control, is separately displayed with its true time.
The six failure-state labels agree with retained raw records.

Before video encoding, all1,715 representative and116 failed-prefix control
frames were rendered from saved true state15 and matched the original collector
RGB hashes exactly. MP4 files are compressed viewing copies, not training ground
truth or a claim of lossless encoded pixels. The PNG strips and main on-demand
RGB interface retain original pixel values. Rendering advanced no physics and
used no model or checkpoint.

The control-time curves expose a real limitation: TRAIN H1 obvious-motion
fractions are54.59% for anchors3–21,0.65% for22–29 and28.83% for30–47. These
are observed time bins; release/recontact scenario phase timings vary. Later
pushes include substantial stalls, which are retained. Signed-rotation medians
near zero aggregate mixed signs and static/small transitions; separate sign
support is reported rather than treating a signed median as absence of motion.

This is a review of recorded real data and its presentation. It provides no
evidence yet that new data or clean/weak conditioning improves model predictions.
