## Failure mechanism and implemented contract

An infinite-mass kinematic pusher can request motion that is incompatible with a dynamic object constrained against walls. More solver iterations alone cannot make the commanded boundary motion feasible. The new module retains the original100Hz PD request and10Hz absolute target action, but executes the kinematic velocity through unilateral geometric gap-rate constraints at fixed1000Hz integration. It is explicitly a new execution/integration protocol, not exact original parameter/step parity.

Each.001s microstep constrains pusher center to[22,489]^2. At the nearest union boundary, with outward normal n, signed circle gap g, body surface velocity u and compression allowance e=.25worldpx, require n·v >= n·u−(g+e)/dt. Intersect with |v|<=|v_PD| and choose the closest velocity to the PD request. This is solved by finite analytic line/line-circle candidate enumeration. Tangential movement remains possible. An infeasible linearized command executes zero velocity and is disclosed; physical solver still advances the dynamic body. No object velocity/position is rewritten.

The small allowed compression establishes genuine native collision impulse, so motion is not prevented at first contact. Blocked wall approach saturates instead of indefinitely driving into solid. Supplemental legality checks reject any>1worldpx overlap (0.1875 imagepx), nonfinite state, center in solid, or externally entering a sealed hole. Geometry comes from the unmodified actual union, not RGB masks. The1world tolerance is less than6% of the thinnest B stroke; epsilon=.25 is0.046875 imagepx. Old3.6–4.35 overlaps remain old observations, not silently accepted or erased. Guarding alone is not counted as repair.
