Inside the original PushT Diffusion Policy

Actual recorded DDPM samples from the frozen T-trained policy. No world model or policy training is used.

The dots are proposed pusher targets, not predicted object positions. The network conditions on two images and two pusher positions. Reward is measured for analysis; it is not an input to this policy.

During denoising, the input scene stays fixed. Physical execution uses the final sample and then shows the measured simulator frames. The policy plans 16 targets; only indices 1-8 are executed before replanning.

Blue: coverage. Orange: IoU (intersection over union). Dashed line: success requires coverage above 95%. Green shading: selected action chunk. Red line: earliest best IoU. The chart includes the full saved episode, beyond the diagnostic replay window.

This reveals the sampled decision sequence, not a causal explanation of individual neural features. The hold comparison is a posthoc intervention using the known best moment; it is not a deployable stopping rule. Data and images are embedded, so this page works offline without a server.
